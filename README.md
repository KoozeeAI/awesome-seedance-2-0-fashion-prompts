## 🧠 What is Seedance 2.0?

Seedance 2.0 is an AI video generation model. For fashion teams, it is relevant to campaign motion, apparel and accessory showcases, product commercials, model movement, and short-form fashion video concepts.

### Common Fashion Use Cases

- Fashion campaign films
- Product commercials
- Apparel and accessory showcases
- Fashion lookbook motion
- AI model movement
- Short-form social advertising
