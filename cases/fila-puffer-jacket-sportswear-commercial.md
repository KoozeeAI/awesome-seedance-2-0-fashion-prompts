# FILA Puffer Jacket Sportswear Commercial

A curated AI fashion prompt for Outerwear · Sportswear fashion commercial · Video.

## Prompt

Create a 15-second high energy luxury fashion commercial  featuring a stylish young woman wearing a white, black, and red color block FILA puffer jacket with a high stand collar, front zipper, quilted padding, and iconic chest logo. Film inside a premium fashion studio with a clean white seamless backdrop, professional softbox lighting, dramatic rim lights, and subtle haze for cinematic depth.

Shot on ARRI Alexa 35, Cooke Anamorphic lenses, ultra-realistic 4K HDR, 120fps slow motion for selected moments, crisp editorial color grading, shallow depth of field, premium sportswear campaign aesthetic.

Sequence

0–2 sec
Rapid macro cuts of the jacket:

 Close-up of the FILA logo
 Zipper being pulled up
 Puffy quilted texture
 Sleeve color blocking
 Light gliding across the fabric

2–5 sec
The model throws the jacket onto her shoulders in one fluid motion, then quickly zips it up. Fast dolly push-in with dynamic lighting transitions.

5–8 sec
Confident runway walk toward the camera. Quick alternating shots:

 Full-body tracking shot
 Low-angle hero shot
 Side profile
 Close-up of the jacket moving naturally

8–12 sec
Quick fashion poses:

 Adjusting the collar
 Hands in pockets
 Looking over the shoulder
 Half spin
 Slow-motion hair movement
 Camera circles around the model

12–15 sec
Hero ending. The model stops in the center of the studio, confidently facing the camera. Slow push-in as the jacket fills the frame. End with a clean luxury brand reveal and space for the FILA logo.

Style

Luxury sportswear campaign, modern streetwear fashion, premium studio production, Vogue editorial, energetic editing, fast-paced cuts every 0.5–1 second, cinematic lighting, ultra-realistic fabric texture, dramatic highlights, smooth gimbal movement, subtle handheld energy, premium commercial quality, stylish, youthful, confident, visually striking.

Audio

Modern upbeat electronic instrumental with cinematic bass and rhythmic beats. No voiceover. No dialogue. No ambient sounds. No subtitles. Music synchronized with every fast cut for maximum impact.
