## Prompt

cinematic luxury bridal fashion runway film, set on an enchanting outdoor garden runway inspired by vintage European cottagecore aesthetics. The entire runway is surrounded by lush greenery, blooming white and pastel flowers, wildflowers, climbing roses, tall elegant trees, soft grass and delicate butterflies naturally flying through the scene. The atmosphere feels like an expensive vintage European garden fashion show, romantic, dreamy, timeless and slightly magical.

A breathtaking female haute couture bridal model walks confidently and gracefully toward the camera. She wears an extraordinary custom-made PURE WHITE wedding gown, entirely white with absolutely no colored fabric. The gown is crafted from luxurious silk, delicate translucent tulle, fine couture lace and layered flowing fabric, completely embellished with thousands of tiny sparkling crystal and diamond-like details. Intricate white-on-white embroidery covers the bodice, with elegant off-shoulder details, a sculpted couture silhouette and an enormous cathedral-length flowing train.

She wears an extravagant diamond necklace, matching diamond earrings, an elegant sparkling bridal tiara and a long translucent white veil. Her face is ethereally beautiful and almost unreal, with perfectly symmetrical high-fashion features, flawless yet realistic skin texture, natural expression and sophisticated bridal elegance.

0–4s: Wide cinematic establishing shot. The bride emerges from a charming ivy-covered garden entrance and slowly begins walking along the outdoor runway. The runway is surrounded by abundant flowers, ancient trees, lush greenery and butterflies gently flying around her. Vintage European cottagecore atmosphere, soft morning sunlight filtering through the trees, subtle film grain, dreamy natural haze.

4–8s: The camera smoothly tracks backward in front of her, capturing her entire body. Her pure white couture gown and enormous cathedral train flow naturally with every step. The silk, tulle and lace move realistically in the gentle garden breeze. Thousands of crystals catch the sunlight and sparkle naturally. Small butterflies occasionally pass through the foreground.

8–12s: The camera slowly moves closer from a medium shot into an elegant close-up, revealing her ethereal face, sparkling diamond necklace, tiara, translucent veil and extremely intricate white crystal embroidery. Soft sunlight creates beautiful highlights across the diamonds while flowers and greenery become creamy bokeh in the background. She maintains a calm, elegant and confident expression.

12–15s: The camera gently moves slightly around her as she reaches the end of the garden runway and makes a graceful subtle turn. The enormous white cathedral train sweeps beautifully across the flower-covered path, revealing its full dramatic scale. Butterflies flutter around the train as sunlight shines through the translucent veil. A breathtaking final luxury bridal fashion moment, like a vintage European couture film.
